Contract summary:
- `VerifiedBinanceArchiveCache` streams checksum and ZIP transfers under a transfer cap, verifies SHA-256 before atomic sidecar/ZIP renames, and parses the sole CSV entry under independent ZIP and CSV caps.
- Cache paths are derived only from the fixed Binance symbol and date key. Per-key async locks serialize same-archive work; quota/LRU state is separately serialized and initializes from the flat local cache directory.
- `CachePolicy::Bounded` rejects an oversized verified candidate before any quota eviction. Retrieval, checksum, ZIP, CSV, and cache failures surface as typed `DataSourceError::ReplayGap`; CEX rows are never stored in Turso.
