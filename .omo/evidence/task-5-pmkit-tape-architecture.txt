Schema and identity summary:
- PM envelopes are scoped by `(portfolio_id, run_id)` and deduplicated by
  `(portfolio_id, run_id, source_id, connection_id, source_timestamp_ms, ingest_sequence)`.
- Cursor pages are static parameterized queries ordered by
  `(source_timestamp_ms, ingest_sequence)` within an owner scope.
- Raw frame bytes and normalized JSON each store SHA-256 digests; unsupported
  versions or invalid digests replay as typed `ReplayGap`s.
- Causal decisions and durable pending intents share
  `(portfolio_id, run_id, correlation_id, source_timestamp_ms, ingest_sequence)`.
- Pending intents transition exactly once to accepted, rejected, or unknown.
- Local database files are owner-only on Unix and deletion removes database,
  WAL, and SHM files; no CEX rows or credentials are persisted.
